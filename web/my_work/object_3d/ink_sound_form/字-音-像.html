Reading guide

Read from right to left, or follow the ink with your pointer or fingertip. Select Stop reading to keep a passage. Build 3D form folds its notes and paths into a sphere. Double-click to gather the particles into a still form. Double-click again to scatter them and resume rotation. Drag to rotate and scroll to zoom.

Long strokes sound low (C3) and short strokes sound high (C7), with semitone steps between. Thicker strokes last longer. Pale ink sounds brighter, and broken ink shortens the gap before the next note. Connected strokes sound together an octave lower. Reverb controls the decay. The five descriptors show length, width, ink tone, speed and density. Uploaded images use the same scale. The camera captures after three seconds. Export the form as OBJ or the score as WAV.
